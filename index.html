Our Story

Rudra Solutions didn’t start in a glass-walled corporate boardroom. Founded in 2026 on the streets of Hyderabad, starting from zero with nothing more than two-wheelers, relentless cold calls, and face-to-face pitches, our agency was built on one simple belief: most businesses don’t suffer from a lack of “clicks”; they suffer from broken funnels and disconnected execution.

Today, led by our CEO, Edulakanti Akshitha, alongside our dedicated outreach and creative specialists, we operate as an agile, speed-first execution partner rather than a slow, traditional agency.
